# Steven Ammer

I build software with AI agents. I write the specs, I try to break what comes back, and I own the risk decisions. I've spent 15 years in consumer phone sales as an agent, supervisor and trainer, and I still work on the phones. I also wrote C in college.

I'm learning in public, so some of this is rough on purpose.

## Featured

**[Interview Coach](https://github.com/stevenammer/interview-coach)**
An open-source app for practicing interviews with AI feedback. The sample workspace is my own practice for a training role, drafts and gaps included. MIT licensed.

**[Systems Thinking for Sales](https://github.com/stevenammer/systems-thinking-for-sales)**
Ten short essays that apply bottlenecks, queues, feedback loops and delays to a phone sales floor. All the examples are invented. CC BY 4.0.

**[Before the Vibes](https://github.com/stevenammer/before-the-vibes)**
The C programs I wrote in 2013 and 2014, published as they were, bugs and all, with an honest review of what they do and don't show. The finished word search is still missing.

## How I work

- I don't write most of the code now. Agents do. My job is to say what done looks like, test it hard, and decide what's safe to ship.
- I would rather publish the rough version and say what's wrong with it than polish it until nobody can check it.

## Not here

My employer's work, anyone else's data, and anything I haven't checked.
